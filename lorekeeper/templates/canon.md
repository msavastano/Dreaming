# Canon

## Story so far
The adventure has not begun. The hero arrives at dusk in Thornwick, a farming
village on the edge of the Greywood, carrying a letter for the village elder.

## Open threads
- The sealed letter for Elder Maren. The hero does not know what it says.
- Livestock have gone missing near the Greywood for three nights running.

## What the party knows
- Thornwick's inn is the Crooked Lantern, run by Hobb, a talkative halfling.
- The old road north through the Greywood has been closed since the spring floods.

## World rules
- Magic is rare and costly. Every spell leaves the caster tired or marked.
- The dead do not stay quiet in the Greywood after moonrise.

## How to use memory
- Stay consistent with this canon and with earlier turns. If unsure, keep details vague rather than inventing a contradiction.
